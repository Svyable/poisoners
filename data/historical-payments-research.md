# POISONERS — historical environmental monetary-outcomes research audit
_As of 2026-11-07 · Source-led evidence inventory · Public-record research, not a quantification of harm_

## Scope and question

Which monetary outcomes involving companies in the 50-company climate-cohort can be documented from public government, court, statutory or issuer sources **as already paid**, as opposed to ordered, agreed, reserved, estimated, spent on works, or still subject to installment schedules? The input is a **legal event**, not a company-harm score or an inventory of all company payments. The source ledger is [`docs/damages-data.json`](../docs/damages-data.json), published in [the Payments Ledger](../docs/damages.html).

**Current coverage:** 42 source-linked monetary matters, including six records in this pass (five fully confirmed paid amounts and one dated partial payment) and three independent additional Volkswagen/Bosch/3M records concurrently introduced on main since the previous 33-record research round. Twenty-four of these matters explicitly link to eleven names from the 50-company cohort (ExxonMobil, Chevron, ConocoPhillips, Occidental Petroleum, Suncor Energy, ONGC, Canadian Natural Resources, Cenovus Energy, Coal India, EOG Resources, Petrobras); the other **39** company profiles have no indexed matter in this *limited ledger*. Non-inclusion never means zero penalties, environmental damage, or no payments.

## Purchasing-power normalization (not an inflation-based legal award)

- All comparisons use **2025 purchasing power in the original currency**, never currency conversion or a global-dollar leaderboard.
- **USD:** U.S. CPI-U annual mean (1982–84=100), 2025 **321.943**, [BLS historical annual indices](https://www.bls.gov/regions/northeast/data/consumerpriceindex_us_table.htm).
- **BRL:** existing IBGE IPCA 2022–2025 compounded year-over-year Dec/Dec method, applied only to the 2021 BRL case. This is not a judicial indexation.
- **CAD:** Canada-wide CPI all-items annual mean (2002=100), [Statistics Canada table 18-10-0005-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810000501) and [BC Stats reproduction of Statistics Canada historical annual levels](https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/2c75c627-3eb6-41ee-bb54-7b089eade484/resource/93e4367b-56af-4e1c-aea7-48fb48f0727c/download/cpi_annual_averages.pdf): 2015 **126.6**, 2019 **136.0**, 2022 **151.2**, 2024 **160.9**, 2025 **164.2**.
- **INR:** all-India CPI Combined (2012=100), calendar-year means obtained by averaging twelve published monthly index levels, not a yearly rate of inflation. Government base table: [India Ministry of Statistics PIB 2025 release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2110779&lang=2&reg=48); 2025 months compiled from [Reserve Bank of India Table 163](https://rbi.org.in/SCRIPTs/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23988). Annual values: 2022 **172.150**, 2023 **181.883**, 2024 **190.883**, 2025 **195.083**.
- **Nominal disposition-year comparator:** obligation amount × CPI(2025) ÷ CPI(disposition year).
- **Actual paid-cash comparator:** if a **known payment year** exists, paid amount × CPI(2025) ÷ CPI(payment year); if *all* dated cash tranches are available and reconcile to the reported paid total, sum each independently indexed tranche. Otherwise **NOT COMPUTED**, not a made-up cash year. An exact payment date is not claimed merely because an enforcement notice documents the fine.
- **Worked ONGC example:** (₹75,210,000 × 195.083/172.150) + (₹152,452,500 × 195.083/181.883) = **₹248,745,724** approximately, or **₹24.875 crore in 2025 purchasing power**. The nominal cash total remains **₹22.76625 crore**; no interest, investment return or exchange-rate adjustment has been applied.
- Calendar-year annual averages are not a legal indexation under tribunal orders. Headline numbers rounded for display; underlying record retains exact nominal amounts. Aggregating across USD/CAD/INR/BRL is prohibited.

## Evidence rules for the next tranche of research

For each matter, record legal entity, current cohort identity link, time-bound ownership, original legal amount, **monetary category**, legal year, exact or approximate paid amount, paid-year if independently established, whether the source explicitly uses “paid/received,” source publication date, source URL/document locator, currency base, all double-counting relationships and any payments that are only a portion of a larger settlement. Mark missing data null, not zero. Review statutory text for actual receipt and trustee reports for restricted funds. The threshold to mark a payment **confirmed_paid** is an explicit source statement about disbursement, not an unsatisfied sentence or estimated cleanup cost.

The 2024 Canadian White Rose case illustrates the discipline: a federal retrospective describes a **C$2.5m sentence**, but the cited offence record does not establish full payment. The figure belongs in *obligated*, not in *paid*. The site and Rank Lab must continue to compare only one original currency and one accounting boundary at a time.

### 2000 Brazilian-real purchasing-power calculations

The existing Brazil IPCA 2021–2025 comparator is now extended back through the **2000 nominal/payment base year** without translating reais into dollars. Official [IBGE historical national IPCA December/December annual rates](https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/economicas/precos-e-custos/9256-indice-nacional-de-precos-ao-consumidoramplo.html?edicao=20932&t=series-historicas) and [Ipeadata series](https://www.ipeadata.gov.br/ExibeSerie.aspx?module=M&serid=1410807112) provide annual changes: 2001 7.67%, 2002 12.53%, 2003 9.30%, 2004 7.60%, 2005 5.69%, 2006 3.14%, 2007 4.46%, 2008 5.90%, 2009 4.31%, 2010 5.91%, 2011 6.50%, 2012 5.84%, 2013 5.91%, 2014 6.41%, 2015 10.67%, 2016 6.29%, 2017 2.95%, 2018 3.75%, 2019 4.31%, 2020 4.52%, 2021 10.06%, 2022 5.79%, 2023 4.62%, 2024 4.83%, 2025 4.26%.

A **2000-to-2025 factor of approximately 4.397831** therefore gives:
- **Guanabara paid fine:** R$35,700,000 × 4.397831 ≈ **R$157,002,555 in 2025 reais**.
- **Paraná paid fine:** R$40,000,000 × 4.397831 ≈ **R$175,913,226 in 2025 reais**.
- Both values are same-currency *purchasing-power illustrations*, not court interest, environmental injury, current debts or US-dollar equivalents.
- U.S. historical EOG figures use the sourced BLS 2010 annual CPI-U **218.056** and 2017 **245.120**, each divided into 2025 **321.943**, while 2025 cash itself has factor 1. Coal India's cash-year CPI remains null because an administrative reporting year is not a cash transaction year.
