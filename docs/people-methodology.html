PEOPLE RESEARCH STANDARD / V4.0 · NOVEMBER 2026

Proof does not
come with a title.

This is the operating method for connecting disclosed individuals to industrial decisions without confusing executive compensation, company-level emissions, personal responsibility and legally actionable harm.

01 — Ten functions, not ten accused people.

Every one of the 50 company records has exactly ten research functions, such as executive leadership, finance, operations, environmental management, facility operation, legal/compliance, strategy, board oversight and historical predecessors. There are 500 source-research positions in total. A position is only populated with an individual's name after a primary public filing identifies the person and their disclosed corporate role.

The launch covers 34 publicly disclosed named officers across seven U.S.-listed companies, sourced to annual SEC proxy or Form 10-K/A disclosures for fiscal 2025. The remaining 466 role positions are explicitly unverified and must not be read as 466 concealed individuals. One officer can legitimately hold multiple functions; ten functions do not imply ten distinct people. We do not fill unverified positions with unconfirmed social-media biographies or inferred family members.

No personal harm ranking has been computed. An officer named in a company’s compensation disclosure is not a pollution defendant or evidence of wrongdoing. The launch does not link named people to specific facilities, acts, exposures, personal injuries or individual illicit gain.

02 — SEC-reported pay is not a person's wealth or profit from pollution.

Public U.S. executive compensation can be analyzed under the ExxonMobil 2026 proxy, Chevron 2026 proxy, Occidental 2026 proxy, Peabody 2026 proxy, EOG 2026 proxy, ConocoPhillips 2026 proxy and Devon 2025 Form 10-K/A.

Each displayed dollar figure is the 2025 SEC Summary Compensation Table total: it may combine salary, bonuses, non-equity incentive payments, equity awards valued at grant rather than realized, certain noncash perquisites, and actuarial pension-value changes. Those amounts are not equivalent to cash taken home, realized gains, net worth, beneficial ownership, proceeds of allegedly harmful decisions or environmental damages caused. Ranking pay disclosure amounts does not rank environmental misconduct.

Comparison rule: the UI sorts only fiscal-2025 figures reported in U.S. dollars under an SEC compensation framework, with the reporting year and source prominently displayed. It does not compare pay to company climate emissions as an individual guilt ratio. For non-U.S. issuers, comparability across differing currencies, filing regimes, accounting standards and reporting periods requires separate source-specific normalization.

03 — An actual responsibility timeline needs dated decisions.

An officer's job title is a starting lead. To examine an individual during a relevant event, require verified effective role start/end dates and date-bound corporate control relationships. If a particular date has not been checked, the field remains unknown rather than implying continuous authority. Historical officers matter: current company websites are not a substitute for an officer list during the release period.

Next, assemble a specific, primary-supported decision trail: facility permit and operator identity; actual authority over waste handling, discharges or environmental compliance; relevant meetings or instructions where legally public; regulatory correspondence; dated internal decisions made available through lawful processes; and counterevidence or limits on the person's authority.

04 — Emissions do not establish an individual's act or injury.

Public name → dated mandate → facility / asset → documented decision or omission → substance / release → actual exposure → scientifically supported outcome → relevant legal element → independent counsel

Evidence about sold-product greenhouse-gas attribution to a producer does not establish that a named board member personally operated a toxic-waste site. Compensation and board committee membership cannot substitute for pollutant-specific site records or exposure studies. A future scientific model must distinguish individual acts, organizational policy, other sources of emissions, latency, alternative causes and the affected receptor.

Every individual record carries unknown for alleged personal pollution profits, attributable health injury and court-actionable wrongdoing until evidence genuinely supports a relevant determination. These are missing quantities, not zero environmental harm or immunity.

05 — Personal liability in the United States: begin with the element, not the target.

For sites within the United States, EPA's CERCLA guidance identifies current owners/operators, past owners/operators at disposal time, certain disposal arrangers and certain transporters as potentially responsible party classes. To apply a particular category, one must establish a qualifying hazardous substance, site, release, response costs, conduct, and defenses. This does not create a rule that every senior officer or shareholder at a polluting company is individually liable.

The U.S. Supreme Court's decision in United States v. Bestfoods, 524 U.S. 51 (1998), distinguishes general corporate ownership and oversight from direct operation of a facility's pollution-related activities. It considers operation connected with leakage, disposal or compliance decisions, not a company's financial success alone. This is a key screening lens, not a personalized defendant finding.

Other possible theories—state-law negligence, trespass, nuisance, property damage or properly brought shareholder derivative litigation—have jurisdiction-specific standing, duty, causation, remedies, procedural, defenses and time limits. Shareholder derivative remedies generally concern harm to the company and should not be confused with injury claims by affected residents. An executive's salary cannot establish these elements.

Before filing any pleading or publishing an adverse allegation, qualified counsel must independently assess factual support, legality, jurisdiction, applicable statute, personal jurisdiction, procedural requirements, and defenses. For U.S. federal civil proceedings, Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 11 imposes factual and legal inquiry obligations. The site never drafts individualized accusations or authorizes filing.

06 — Ten countries, ten distinct legal systems.

The original company atlas includes firms grouped by home country for the United States, Canada, China, India, Russia, Indonesia, Brazil, Iran, Japan and Saudi Arabia. Company home country does not determine the court, law, exposure location, territorial scope of a claim or permissible group litigation process. Environmental liability, shareholder liability, company-person separation and civil remedies differ materially by system.

The public People Atlas deliberately does not offer a universal cross-border class action against each named officer. Before any jurisdiction-specific legal recommendation, local counsel must review the facility, release, alleged personally wrongful conduct, injured legal interest, rights to sue, jurisdiction, service, available remedies, local public records and limitation rules. See the ten-country legal research architecture.

07 — Review, response and privacy.

A person's public executive identity and regulator-linked professional compensation may be used for factual, source-attributed research, but the person must not be portrayed as criminally or civilly responsible solely by inclusion. Every source record identifies its origin and reporting period. Material disputed content requires clear labeling, evidence-based correction and a right-to-respond process, with an audit trail.

Do not collect or publish personal home addresses, phone numbers, personal emails, family relationships, medical histories, private holdings, confidential legal communications or claimant data. No public court service guides, pressure campaigns, targeted contact lists or private investigator tactics are included. GitHub issues accept public documentary records only.

08 — The next research milestones are objective.

MilestoneWhat establishes completion
P0 / Source-backed namesVerified annual filing, legal names and roles; 34 officers seeded, with 466 role slots explicitly unverified.
P1 / TenureDated appointment and departure documents for each relevant officer and company.
P2 / AuthorityTime-valid legal entity and actual facility/operator control; committee mandate alone is insufficient.
P3 / DecisionsDocumented specific action or omission linked to a particular environmental release.
P4 / ExposureMeasured substance and medium, fate/transport, recipient population or ecosystem, uncertainty.
P5 / HarmIndependently reviewed counterfactual causation and actual remedy-linked loss, or explicit null result.
P6 / CounselSeparate jurisdiction and law analysis by qualified counsel, evidence review and deliberate authorization.

Each milestone is falsifiable. A failed investigation, corrected source or conclusion that no individual legal claim exists is a valid research outcome. More names or larger dollar amounts are not substitutes for stronger evidence.

i

Public officers are not accused persons. A job title, company affiliation or disclosed compensation amount does not establish environmental injury, individual wrongdoing, causation or legal liability. These are research leads subject to source verification and due process.
