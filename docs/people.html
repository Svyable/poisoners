03 / INDIVIDUAL RESPONSIBILITY · COUNSEL TRIAGE

Evidence first.
Legal theory second.

A legally viable personal claim requires more than a title or the size of a paycheck. Review the evidence requirements for several legal research paths, then build a public-source briefing—not an automated lawsuit.

FULL LEGAL & PUBLICATION STANDARD ↗
NO AUTO-FILING. NO UNFOUNDED DEFENDANTS. The website neither determines that any listed executive caused environmental damage nor provides an individual accusation or filing strategy. U.S. CERCLA site-operator liability requires evidence of pollution-related operations; other countries' rules require separate local legal analysis.
THE NEXT STANDARD OF PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY

Know the person.
Prove the act.
Establish the harm.

Research must connect an identifiable person, a specific decision or omission, an actual facility and release, a dated exposure pathway, a supported outcome, and an applicable cause of action. Those links are investigated—not presumed.
