01 — The product is evidence infrastructure.

POISONERS is designed to connect environmental events, reported pollutant releases, modeled transport, chemical potency, human and ecological exposure, outcome estimates and legal-remedy research. A link in this chain is not assumed merely because a chemical is hazardous or a company operates in the region.

Company / legal entity → asset / facility → activity → agent → release → medium → transport → exposure → receptor → endpoint → claim / uncertainty → independent review

The public observatory begins with company production-linked greenhouse gases. The expanded architecture defines 142 potential metrics across 14 scientific, regulatory and social domains; these are questions we can investigate, not 142 newly measured company impacts. The source register currently contains 22 research entrypoints; most are links to external data rather than imported or redistributable datasets.

02 — A normalized research data graph.

The architecture separates legal entities, subsidiaries, owners, facilities, substances, releases, measurements, geospatial locations, transport models, exposure cohorts, outcomes, uncertainty bounds, jurisdiction-specific legal matters, reviewers and citations. Each edge has effective dates and document-level provenance.

Raw source documents are retained only when licenses and privacy law permit. Publicly published claims point to sources even if the source itself cannot be redistributed. No personal claimant records belong in this public repository.

03 — The Index is not a crude single score.

A defensible system should expose a tensor of time × media × receptor × agent × endpoint × geography × responsibility boundary × evidence quality. Examples include a 2024 air PM2.5 release in kilograms, local ambient concentration in micrograms per cubic meter, modeled respiratory burden in cases, or a verified court judgment. They answer different questions and do not share a summation unit.

Index(entity, year) = { H, A, F, E, R, Q, J } × { air, water, soil, plants, animals, humans } × { hazard family, endpoint, place }

H = estimated historical burdens, A = current-year burden, F = conditional future scenarios, E = exposure distribution and vulnerability, R = persistence and reversibility, Q = evidence readiness/quality, J = jurisdiction and potential remedies. There is no automatic cross-unit “total harm” number. Any later composite must make normative weights, uncertainty, counterfactuals and rank instability public.

Data completeness is not impact magnitude. Fifty cited GHG producer observations can support an emissions table. They cannot support fifty corporate deaths-per-year figures without independent causal models. Similarly, a large settlement amount cannot be interpreted as a toxicity measurement.

04 — Seven gates, with stop conditions.

GatePassing condition
G0 / AcquisitionVerifiable source, legal re-use rights, source date, exact table or document locator.
G1 / IdentitySource-matched company and facility identifiers, ownership valid during observation year.
G2 / MeasurementPollutant identity, explicit mass/concentration, uncertainty, time window and medium.
G3 / ExposureTransport model or measured pathways, population/ecological baselines, local confounders.
G4 / OutcomeEndpoint and counterfactual, dose response, sensitivity, independent scientific review.
G5 / PublicationFact-check, attribution language, correction/rebuttal channel, privacy and defamation review.
G6 / LegalIndependent qualified counsel's jurisdiction, class/representative action and remedy review; separate authorization before action.

Fail closed. A missing G2–G4 pathway produces “not estimated,” never zero, never a made-up index number and never a published allegation of injury. Conflicting evidence becomes “disputed” with an audit trail.

05 — A repeatable operating cycle.

  1. Discover. Identify authoritative source updates and obtain permitted data exports; note whether reporting vintages are preliminary.
  2. Fingerprint. Record retrieval time, source URLs, file hashes, licenses and schema versions.
  3. Normalize. Resolve legal entities, facilities, dates, CAS numbers and units; never perform unreviewed fuzzy parent matches.
  4. Reconcile. Detect overlapping Scope 1 and sold-product Scope 3 emissions, recycled transfers, duplicate facilities, repeated cases, and boundaries before any aggregation.
  5. Model. Run only published or independently reviewable scientific transport/exposure/dose-response models.
  6. Challenge. Attempt to disconfirm findings; solicit relevant technical correction and allow for a documented source response.
  7. Publish. Update only reviewed, licensing-compliant, source-linked data; record version changes and uncertainties.
  8. Refer. Provide counsel with restricted, authenticated case dossiers where appropriate; do not automate claims or solicitations.

The repository's deterministic build, regression tests and dry-run evidence importer implement the first safety layer. They do not substitute for experts or a secure claims-management backend.

06 — Ten countries, different sources, same standard.

Our current ten-country cohort was selected by published 2025 EDGAR territorial GHG totals for sovereign countries. It is not the ten nations with greatest total toxic harm. Every country gets ten target company dossier slots, but a credible dossier requires much more than a Carbon Majors entry. For coverage accountability, even countries with more than ten seeded entities are capped at ten filled **emissions-only research slots**.

Examples of source-first country investigation: US EPA ECHO and TRI; Canada's NPRI; Japan's PRTR; China's discharge permits; Brazil's IBAMA enforcement data; and India's NGT judgments. These have distinct content and accessibility limits; some are legal records, not chemical-release databases.

Country workstreams are visible in the public explorer. The website reports zero validated multi-hazard dossiers because it has not yet done causal pollutant and outcome attribution. That is an explicit integrity feature, not missing UI.

07 — Scientific evidence and legal action are different tracks.

A regulatory notice is not necessarily a final adjudication. A toxic substance released is not proof a particular person encountered it. A source-producer attributed climate total is not automatically evidence of a class-certifiable private injury. Counsel must independently assess local standing, group certification, individualized causation, limitation periods, alternative remedies and sovereignty issues.

Public GitHub issues are for published documents and citation corrections only, not whistleblower identities, vulnerable-community contact lists, medical histories or confidential legal advice. The case intake envisioned for later phases must be privately operated with informed consent, regional privacy compliance, access control, threat modeling and attorney oversight. See the legal architecture.

08 — Provenance, fairness and licensing are part of accuracy.

InfluenceMap's Carbon Majors bulk dataset is distributed under noncommercial-use conditions. The current public seed is a documented subset of the published briefing, not a mirrored bulk download. Bulk imports, commercial sublicensing, derivative resale or private redistributions require permissions and a legal/license assessment.

For materially adverse company-level publication, describe facts precisely, attribute allegations to sources, report final versus disputed legal outcomes, log corrections publicly and offer an evidence-based right to respond. Never present modeled exposure as observed illness or use settlement dollars as an injury proxy.

09 — Objective milestones rather than vanity coverage.

MilestoneExit criteria
M0 / ObservatoryPublic site, 50 source-linked climate-only records, 142 defined metrics, 22 catalogued sources and automated integrity tests.
M1 / Facility layerTen or more independently cross-verified facility IDs; dated owners, pollutant-specific reported releases and exact source citations.
M2 / PathwaysOne replicable source→receptor transport/exposure dossier in each of at least two different countries and pollutants; documented uncertainties.
M3 / OutcomesExpert-reviewed attributable-health and ecological endpoints with counterfactual and uncertainty, including failed or null studies.
M4 / Country scaleTen genuinely qualified company dossiers in each cohort country, or explicit published failure to find adequate evidence.
M5 / RemediesCounsel-led, jurisdiction-specific evaluation; no filing or outreach without informed, separately authorized human decisions.

A capable accountability system must also record what it cannot establish. That discipline is what makes future research, public understanding and legitimate remedies more credible.
