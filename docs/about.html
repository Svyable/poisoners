01 / WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS

Make environmental claims inspectable.

Climate inventories, industrial release records, health studies and legal proceedings describe different parts of the same world. Each uses its own units, entities, dates and standard of proof. POISONERS is an open research effort to make those boundaries intelligible and connect them responsibly.

The proposed Global Poison Index is a multidimensional research design for historical, annual and modeled future environmental harm—not a finished scoring system and not a court of public opinion.

Its purpose is to make a better question possible: What evidence would we need before drawing this conclusion?

03 / HOW WE WORK

Public data does not waive the burden of proof.

01

Every record has provenance

Trace figures to named sources, vintages, boundaries and transformations, and correct errors in the open.

02

Unknown is not zero

Absent exposure, health and liability evidence stays absent rather than being replaced by an invented composite score.

03

Scientific and legal gates remain separate

Scientific causal inference requires reviewed methods. Allegations, claimant outreach and court filings require qualified local counsel and separate authorization.

04

Fairness is part of accuracy

Maintain reproducibility, rights to correction and response, privacy boundaries and the distinction between accusations, documented facts and settlements.

04 / FORWARD WORK

Measure progress by verified evidence.

  1. Expand legally reusable facility and pollutant-specific source coverage with provenance and licensing checks.
  2. Resolve company, subsidiary and facility identities with effective-date ownership rather than name-only matching.
  3. Build reviewed exposure and impact models before proposing any scientific harm subindices.
  4. Add independent correction, rebuttal and audit procedures as the research matures.
  5. Keep private claimant material out of this public static site; legal matters belong with appropriate professionals.

See the operating protocol for release gates and the repository for implementation history.

05 / CORRECTIONS AND PARTICIPATION

Disagree with a record? Show the source.

Use the public issue tracker for dated official documents, specific corrections and reproducible data-quality concerns. Do not share private patient details, claimant identities or unverified personal allegations.
