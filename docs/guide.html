01 / THREE GOOD STARTING POINTS

Choose a question. Then choose the right tool.

Each part of POISONERS answers a different kind of question. The responsible answer often is that the current data cannot determine the harm you want to understand.

01

Explore the reported signal

Use the company ledger for source-linked 2024 production-associated greenhouse-gas observations. Filter by company or home country and inspect the attribution boundary before comparing figures.

02

Inspect the individual record

Open Dossier Lab to see source information, missing links and the evidence-gate status. A company record is a research profile, not a completed health or legal dossier.

03

Follow the missing evidence

Use the metric framework, methodology and research protocol to identify the measurements and reviews needed before a stronger conclusion.

02 / DO NOT COLLAPSE DIFFERENT NUMBERS

Three signals. Three different meanings.

The following sources are useful, but they are not interchangeable. Different years, units, geographic definitions and attribution rules prevent a simple combined ranking.

Published signalWhat it supportsWhat it does not establish
Carbon Majors · 2024

Production-linked corporate greenhouse-gas attribution

Year-specific producer estimates and some historical totals using the published accounting boundaryLocal toxic releases, where emissions occurred, illness or legal responsibility
EDGAR · 2025

National territorial greenhouse-gas inventory

Country-level climate emissions under a territorial inventory methodCompany-level allocation or a national ranking of toxic or health harm
Pollution & health review · 2019 burden

Global model-based mortality estimates

Population-level context on pollution's disease burdenDeaths caused by any company or facility in this public register
Never add the national and corporate series together. They may overlap and represent different accounting models. Nor should a global mortality estimate be allocated to individual corporations without separate causal evidence.

03 / FROM OBSERVATION TO ACCOUNTABILITY

What a defensible evidence chain requires.

A reported emission or production estimate can open an investigation. It is not the entire investigation.

01

Identify the source

Record the publication, retrieval date, exact table or record, method, units and uncertainty.

02

Resolve the facility and entity

Establish location and effective-date ownership without assigning one subsidiary's activity to another by name alone.

03

Describe the release and pathway

Distinguish climate gases from water contamination, particles, metals or other pollutant classes; model transport only where supported.

04

Establish exposure and receptor

Assess timing, dose and the actual affected population, species or ecosystem.

05

Test harm and alternatives

Define the outcome and counterfactual, investigate confounders and quantify uncertainty through qualified scientific review.

06

Apply jurisdiction-specific legal tests

Qualified counsel must separately consider standing, causation, time limits and remedies. Findings, allegations and settlements have different meanings.

THE SHORT VERSION

50 public corporate records are source-linked climate observations, not proven toxicity rankings. The ten-country cohort is selected by climate emissions and includes uneven company coverage.

A framework of many metrics is not the same as measured impact. The data is openly inspectable and subject to correction.

04 / FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Common questions, clear boundaries.

Is this a ranking of the world's most toxic companies?

No. The current company set is a source-linked sample from a published production-related climate dataset. A scientific toxic-harm ranking would require pollutant-specific exposure and outcome evidence, reviewed rules, consistent units and uncertainty estimates.

Does a high emissions number prove wrongdoing?

No. Emission quantity, environmental hazard, injury, breach of a legal duty and legal liability are distinct claims. None automatically follows from the first.

Why do company and country figures refer to different years?

The initial company ledger uses Carbon Majors' 2024 producer attribution update, whereas the country atlas uses EDGAR's 2025 territorial inventory. They are displayed with their original dates and must not be treated as one comparable ranking.

Does an empty evidence field mean a company caused no harm?

No. “Not yet assessed” and “not found in this release” are not proof of zero exposure or zero injury. The research gates show missing evidence instead of filling it with assumptions.

Can I use this website as medical or legal advice?

No. It is a public research prototype. Personal environmental-health questions belong with appropriate clinicians and public-health authorities. Legal claims require qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.

How do I report an error or add evidence?

Open a public GitHub issue with a precise source link, date, claimed correction and affected record. Do not upload personal medical information, claimant details, private allegations or confidential legal communications.

05 / CONTRIBUTE WHAT CAN BE CHECKED

Have a source? Make it traceable.

A documented correction is more useful than an unsourced accusation. Include the original URL, date, table or decision and why it changes the record.
