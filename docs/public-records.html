THREE QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU TRUST A MAP

A database is a starting point.

01 / COVERAGE

Is the source included?

Facility thresholds, jurisdiction and sampling locations determine what appears. An empty map is not proof of a clean environment.

02 / VINTAGE

When was it measured?

Current forecasts, annual reports and historical averages cover different periods. Always preserve year, unit and method.

03 / INFERENCE

What does it actually prove?

A reported release, enforcement entry, pollution forecast and measured exposure are not the same kind of evidence.

PUBLIC TOOL GROUP / 01

Global reference

Comparative datasets, not personal or real-time measurements.

World Health Organization

WHO ambient air quality database

What you can find

Annual average PM2.5, PM10 and nitrogen dioxide concentrations for selected settlements.

Coverage & reporting period

City and town measurements from 127 WHO members, associate members or territories; not every settlement. June 2026 version, observations from 2010–2024.

How to start

Find your city, confirm its reporting year and measurement coverage, and compare cautiously.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

A historical city-wide annual mean is not today's local AQI or a personal exposure measurement.

PUBLIC TOOL GROUP / 02

United States

Air conditions, reported releases, compliance and drinking-water reports.

U.S. EPA and partner agencies

AirNow air-quality information

What you can find

AQI levels, smoke information and health-oriented air-quality categories.

Coverage & reporting period

U.S. location-level monitors and forecasts; availability varies by place and pollutant. Current/forecast AQI with real-time data caveats.

How to start

Enter a location and check monitor coverage, forecast time and any advisories.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

A monitor or sensor does not identify all local exposures, individual doses or a responsible facility.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
U.S. Environmental Protection Agency

EPA ECHO facility compliance

What you can find

Facility permit details, inspection records, reported violations and enforcement actions.

Coverage & reporting period

Facilities recorded in applicable EPA and state/tribal environmental reporting systems. Mixed enforcement, permit and reporting windows.

How to start

Search a location or facility ID; read the agency's data definitions and status dates.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

A listed violation can be alleged rather than finally adjudicated; missing records do not prove a facility is safe.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
U.S. Environmental Protection Agency

EPA Toxics Release Inventory

What you can find

Reported chemical releases, waste transfers and pollution-prevention information.

Coverage & reporting period

Facilities and chemicals meeting TRI reporting rules, not every source of pollution. Annual self-reported releases; the EPA toolbox lists current tool status.

How to start

Use EPA's TRI Toolbox to select a current search or download tool; confirm substance, year and reporting thresholds.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

Pounds released are not a measured population dose or toxicity ranking, and some tools may be offline.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
U.S. EPA and local public water systems

Annual drinking-water quality reports

What you can find

Water sources, reported regulated contaminants, violations and supplier contacts.

Coverage & reporting period

Community public-water suppliers; not a private well or a household's individual tap. Annual reports from water suppliers.

How to start

Find your supplier's Consumer Confidence Report, then ask about the location and timing of relevant sampling.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

A supplier-wide report cannot substitute for a qualified lead or other contaminant test at one tap.

PUBLIC TOOL GROUP / 03

Europe

Near-real-time air indicators and declared major industrial releases.

European Environment Agency

European Air Quality Index

What you can find

Short-term air quality estimates for PM2.5, PM10, NO2, ozone and SO2.

Coverage & reporting period

European monitoring stations and modeled air-quality areas; data availability varies. Near-real-time station information and model forecasts.

How to start

Choose a place, distinguish actual stations from modeled areas, and check the timestamp.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

The Index is not a legal compliance determination or a long-term annual air-quality assessment.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
European Environment Agency

European Industrial Emissions Portal

What you can find

Reported industrial releases to air, water and land, transfers of waste and facility details.

Coverage & reporting period

Major industrial complexes under covered European reporting requirements, not all businesses. Annual facility and pollutant reports; check reporting year.

How to start

Find a facility on the map, select a pollutant and reporting year, and read the methodology.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

An annual reported mass does not show local exposure or prove legal responsibility.

PUBLIC TOOL GROUP / 04

Canada

Air-health forecasts and a national industrial emissions inventory.

Environment and Climate Change Canada

Canadian Air Quality Health Index

What you can find

A health-oriented indicator based on air pollutant measurements and forecast conditions.

Coverage & reporting period

Canadian AQHI reporting communities; station and location coverage varies. Current conditions and forecasts.

How to start

Select a community and review the advice for its risk category and the current timestamp.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

A community AQHI is not an individual exposure estimate and local conditions can vary.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
Government of Canada

National Pollutant Release Inventory

What you can find

Facility-reported air, water, and land releases, disposals and transfers.

Coverage & reporting period

Reporting facilities and substances under NPRI thresholds across Canada. 2025 records are labeled preliminary in the current search tool.

How to start

Filter by location, facility or substance and verify whether the year is reviewed or preliminary.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

Preliminary data can contain reporting errors; facility releases are not individual dose measurements.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
PUBLIC TOOL GROUP / 05

United Kingdom

Postcode-based air information and industrial emissions records.

UK Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs

Check air quality in the UK

What you can find

Air quality by locality or postcode, alerts and exposure guidance.

Coverage & reporting period

Area-level air forecasts and available monitors in the UK. Current monitoring and forecasts where provided.

How to start

Search a postcode or place and check the forecast, station data and health message.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

A local forecast is an indicator, not a personal diagnosis or source-attribution model.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
Environment Agency / UK National Data Library

Environment Agency Pollution Inventory

What you can find

Reported annual releases to air and water and waste movements from qualifying sites.

Coverage & reporting period

Regulated major industrial sites in England within the inventory's reporting scope. Downloadable annual data for 2013–2024 at the time of review.

How to start

Download a reporting year and verify facility permit boundaries and substance units.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

Large sites are covered selectively; reported annual masses do not describe personal exposure.

OPEN THE OFFICIAL PORTAL ↗
PUBLIC TOOL GROUP / 06

Australia

Facility and diffuse-source pollutant reports.

Australian Government DCCEEW

Australian National Pollutant Inventory

What you can find

Annual releases and waste transfers of listed NPI pollutants, including facility mapping.

Coverage & reporting period

Australian facilities and diffuse sources within NPI reporting rules. 2024–25 reporting period, updates/corrections possible.

How to start

Select the facilities map or database, then check substance, year and calculation method.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

Different chemicals cannot be summed into a meaningful total-harm ranking; reported release is not confirmed exposure.
