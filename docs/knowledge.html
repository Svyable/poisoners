THE RULE THAT CONNECTS THE LIBRARY

A record is not a conclusion.

Every note states what evidence can support, what cannot be inferred from it, and the next research question. The citations link to agencies, scientific guidance or original data publications—not secondary headlines. Topics are educational entry points, not a ranking of places, people or companies.
