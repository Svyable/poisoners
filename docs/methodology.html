RESEARCH STANDARD / VERSION 2.0

Show your work.

The Poison Index is a proposed analytical system, not a finished scientific instrument. Our first rule: never turn a measurable emission, a modeled exposure, a medical outcome and a legal judgment into the same number.

01 — Model the damage, not the headline.

Every proposed observation must be addressable by entity → legal owner → facility/product → hazardous agent → emitted/released quantity → environmental medium → fate/transport → exposure route → receptor → endpoint → time → jurisdiction → evidence. One company can own many facilities, one facility can release many substances, and one substance can travel through several pathways. We preserve those many-to-many relationships rather than flattening them prematurely.

P(entity) = { H, A, F ; medium × receptor × agent × endpoint ; Q ; J }

H = historical attributable burden, A = annual attributable burden, F = conditional future burden; Q = evidence quality and uncertainty, J = jurisdiction/case suitability. This is a structured set of measures, not yet a single numerical score. Human illness, greenhouse warming, habitat loss and soil contamination cannot be added without a declared valuation framework.

02 — Three temporal horizons.

IndexQuestionRequired inputs
H · HistoricalWhat burden was attributable in past years?Dated releases, ownership at time of activity, background conditions, exposure and outcome evidence, with time lags and uncertainty.
A · AnnualWhat burden is attributable in a specified reporting year?Annual pollutant-specific emissions, concentration/exposure, toxicity and outcome models for that year. Always show reporting-year vintage.
F · FutureWhat burden could occur under stated scenarios?Facility plans and closure dates, projected flows, policy alternatives, transport models, dose-response relationships, persistence and discount assumptions.

The interactive site currently has a 2024 Carbon Majors corporate emissions baseline, a selected historical total for entities in the published table and a user-controlled 2027–2036 emissions-only scenario. None of these are yet a scientifically validated multi-hazard Poison Index. A date mismatch between country inventory (2025) and company inventory (2024) is explicit.

The scenario applies an annual change rate r to a 2024 attributed-emissions value E. It is an exploratory mathematical calculation, not a forward estimate of mortality or ecosystem damage:

Scenario(2027–2036) = Σ [ E(2024) × (1+r)^t ], t = 3,…,12

03 — A multidimensional harm matrix.

Six receptor classes — air, water, soil, plants, animals, humans — intersect eight initial hazard families: climate-forcing gases, combustion pollutants, metals, persistent organics, agrochemicals/nutrients, pharmaceutical actives, biological hazards and plastics/particles. The site labels these cells as research pathways, not observed contamination or scores.

Each validated cell will add dimensions absent from simple pollution charts: chemical identity/CAS number; bioavailability/speciation; chronic versus acute dose; persistence and degradation; bioaccumulation; population vulnerability; geographic transport; critical habitat; reversibility and repair cost; counterfactual alternatives; exposure confidence; and plausible legal causes of action.

Orthogonal dimensions must remain separable. “Air” is an environmental medium, “humans” are a receptor, “lead” is an agent, “neurodevelopmental impairment” is an endpoint, and “2024” is a time slice. Conflating them causes double counting.

04 — From raw records to defensible estimates.

The calculation chain depends on the endpoint. For a chemical: quantify pollutant mass with unit/period, estimate fate and transport, estimate exposed populations or ecosystems, apply an endpoint-specific dose-response method and compare to a supported counterfactual. Human-health outputs might include excess cases, attributable deaths or disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) with confidence intervals. Ecological outputs might include affected habitat area, species/population endpoints, biodiversity indicators or remediation cost — not arbitrary conversion to DALYs.

Never multiply uncalibrated “toxicity × quantity × population” and call it observed damage. For example, an EPA TRI release amount is not automatically an exposure or an adverse health effect; EPA explicitly warns about this distinction. Climate CO₂e values have a different causal structure than local hazardous releases.

A later composite may use published endpoint normalization, stakeholder-validated weights and independent sensitivity analyses, but must always publish the vector subindices and confidence bands. A score must never silently decide that one human life equals a chosen amount of habitat.

05 — Why these ten countries?

The launch cohort selects the ten sovereign countries with the highest 2025 territorial greenhouse-gas emissions in the European Commission JRC EDGAR 2026 report, excluding aggregate regions and international aviation/shipping. In order: China, United States, India, Russia, Indonesia, Brazil, Iran, Japan, Saudi Arabia and Canada. This is an explicit climate-data coverage choice, not an assertion that those nations have the highest overall poison harm.

The ambition is 10 carefully supported company dossiers per country. Coverage is incomplete. The seeded records come from Carbon Majors' published 2024 figures and are associated with a company’s home country for navigation. The source uses worldwide production-linked emissions, including downstream product combustion. Therefore a company’s emission number cannot be subtracted from or directly compared with its home country's territorial figure. Corporate lists are source-covered subsets, not national “worst ten” conclusions.

06 — Grade evidence, not people.

GradeRecord typePublication state
AAuditable primary measurement, official facility data, court-admitted material, independent replicationPublished with source, scope, limits, date and version
BPeer-reviewed or reputable modeled estimate with explicit assumptionsPublished as an estimate, not an observation
CCredible secondary reporting, disclosed company documents, NGO claims with inspectable evidenceLead / corroboration needed; attribute the claim
DUnverified lead or tipNot displayed as established company harm; queue for review

Grade applies to individual claims, not a company overall. Separately track uncertainty, dataset coverage, recency, independence, conflicts of interest and rebuttal responses. Data corrections remain in an auditable log.

07 — Build the ingest pipeline, then automate it.

Suggested sequence: (1) fetch and timestamp authoritative datasets; (2) store raw immutable copies and licenses; (3) normalize pollutant identifiers, units, site locations, corporate parents and reporting periods; (4) deduplicate facility vs supply-chain emissions; (5) geospatially link releases to environmental receptors; (6) run scientific impact models with quantified uncertainty; (7) generate fact-checkable dossiers; (8) conduct scientific and legal review; (9) release publication-ready claims with citations and rebuttal procedures.

Research connectors to prioritize: EPA ECHO for compliance history, EPA TRI for reported releases, EEA industrial releases, Climate TRACE for assets, OpenAQ for ambient measurement, State of Global Air for population exposure and burden, and OECD/UNECE PRTR for multinational register discovery. Respect each dataset's geographical coverage, permit limits, license and uncertainties. In particular, Carbon Majors downloads carry non-commercial terms; obtain permission before commercial bulk reuse.

08 — What this version does not claim.

No named company is here labeled criminal, negligent, a legal defendant, or a proven cause of any identified death or illness. Carbon Majors figures describe attribution of production-linked emissions, not lawbreaking. The 9-million-death Lancet estimate is a global multi-source pollution burden and must not be allocated to the source-linked company subset. Future emissions calculations are deterministic scenarios; they are not predicted harm. Patent-pending status has not been independently verified by this project and should only be represented publicly with filing documentation.

The most valuable next milestone is not a bigger unsourced leaderboard: it is a first pollutant → facility → receptor → outcome dossier, with a reproducible record, a response procedure and a properly scoped expert review.

i

Read the boundary, not just the number. The public company observations are production-linked 2024 climate data; the country atlas uses 2025 territorial estimates. Neither measures proven health injury, toxic-release impact or legal fault.
