KNOW THE COMPOUND.
NOT JUST THE CARBON.

Plants use CO₂ in photosynthesis. That does not make CO₂ irrelevant to climate, but a tonne of CO₂ is not a tonne of arsenic, mercury, sulfur dioxide, or NO₂. This instrument puts respiratory irritants, acid-forming gases, heavy metals, fine particles and food-web toxins in the foreground. Climate forcing stays in its own lane.

03 / COAL-SPECIFIC SOURCE AND RECEPTOR PATHWAYS

Coal is a fuel. Ash is a different chemical problem.

Coal-producing companies, generating utilities, ash repositories and industrial customers are not automatically the same legal entity. Specific chemicals, operations and exposed receptors are needed to establish a defensible chain.

~300regulated coal-ash facilities
~775ash landfills and surface impoundments

EPA's coal-ash enforcement initiative describes these approximate U.S. scope figures; they count regulated locations and units, not releases or proven health injuries. EPA coal ash compliance scope ↗

A / MINING & PROCESSING

Coal, washwater, sediment.

Coal extraction, preparation and ash returned to mines can involve different reporting rules and sources. A coal producer's sale volumes are not a measurement of metals released near a mine.

EPA coal-mining TRI guidance ↗
B / COMBUSTION STACK

SO₂, NOₓ, Hg, acid gases, PM.

Combustion and sulfur capture determine direct gases and particles. SO₂ can form sulfate aerosols; released Hg can later change species and move through food webs. Record stack year, unit and operator separately.

EIA combustion emission figures ↗
C / ASH & WATER

As, Cd, Cr, Pb, Se, Hg.

Fly ash, bottom ash and coal-residual impoundments can affect groundwater and aquatic ecosystems. Metal concentration in ash or groundwater is not automatically a release mass or a causal claim about coal producers.

Stank is not a hazard meter.

Hydrogen sulfide can smell like rotten eggs at tiny concentrations yet suppress the sense of smell at dangerous levels. Mercury, arsenic and lead need no detectable odor to create a serious exposure concern. Odor nuisance, acute toxicity, chronic cancer risk and ecological persistence are four separate dimensions.

Sources: ATSDR hydrogen sulfide ↗ · EPA RSEI interpretation ↗

How a chemical ranking becomes defensible.

A real facility or corporate ranking would first harmonize reporting year, chemical species, unit, release medium and verified owner. Then the next stages add environmental transport, exposure, toxicity weights, vulnerable receptors and uncertainty. EPA's RSEI screening framework can inform relative potential risk for supported TRI records, but it is not proof of causation or a death count.
