03 / COMPARABILITY POLICY

A number is not
a verdict.

01 / ONE UNIT

Currency stays separate.

Legal-payment amounts cannot be ranked across USD and BRL. Select a currency; distinguishing actual disbursement from an ordered amount is essential.

02 / ONE BOUNDARY

Production is not territory.

Company-attributed 2024 GHG estimates and 2025 territorial country inventories have different populations and methods and never share a rank.

03 / ONE COHORT

Chemical masses need their source.

2025 EIA metric tonnes and 2024 EPA short tons use different agencies, years, coverage and units. No joint comparison or per-ton toxicity score.

04 / HONEST GAPS

Unknown stays unranked.

Blank pay, unverified company harms, missing facility evidence and proposed metrics are not zeros. Definitions and research-source counts aren't environmental observations.
