ACCOUNTABILITY SYSTEM / VERSION 2.0

Proof before process.

Scale the work that good environmental litigation requires. Never confuse “many people were exposed” with “this meets the legal test for a class action.” Different countries offer different legal tools.

01 — An evidence factory, not a lawsuit factory.

Each potential matter becomes a structured, time-stamped case dossier linking a legally identifiable entity to particular operations, pollutants, places, affected groups, potential causes of action, possible remedies and source records. A case dossier is an investigative object, not an allegation or draft complaint.

Reusable workflow: Lead → Corroborated → Scientific review → Jurisdiction review → Counsel-approved → Action authorized → Filed / Resolved / Closed. Every transition creates an audit event and specifies which qualified reviewer approved it. An issue can be closed without a claim; this is an intended outcome when evidence is insufficient.

02 — Hard gates before escalation.

GateRequired diligence
IdentityCorrect legal entity, ownership chain and relevant operating period. Parent corporations are not automatically liable for subsidiaries.
ConductDocument actual release or conduct and applicable legal duties; lawful activity may still merit scrutiny but is not necessarily unlawful.
CausationExposure and injury analysis appropriate to the remedy. Account for background sources, latency, alternative causes and uncertainty.
GroupClearly defined affected population; common questions; representative adequacy and conflicts where relevant.
VenueJurisdiction, standing, limitation/repose periods, remedies, forum restrictions, sovereignty defenses and enforceability.
EthicsConfidential claimant handling, informed consent, counsel independence, funding disclosures and no solicitation in breach of local rules.

In the United States, Federal Rule 23 requires numerosity, commonality, typicality and adequacy, plus applicable Rule 23(b) conditions; environmental individual-injury claims may require individualized proof. A coordinated individual action or another remedy can be more appropriate than a class. There is no universal global class action.

These are research starting points, not judgments that a claim is actionable. The underlying law may have amendments, procedural limits or venue-specific rules. All routes require licensed local counsel and a fact-specific review.

Cross-border boundary: an entity's home country is not necessarily the proper court or the location of an affected community. Cross-border suits raise jurisdiction, choice of law, recognition of judgments, sovereign immunity, foreign evidence and cost/funding concerns.

04 — Public data and private people are different systems.

This GitHub Pages prototype is intentionally static. It has no intake form, personal database or claimant sign-up. Do not collect people's names, health conditions, residential addresses, identities, test results or attorney communications in public issues. Future private intake must use access control, encryption, retention limits, documented consent, regional data protections and a qualified legal/clinical review path.

Separate three infrastructures: Public observatory (sourced datasets, methods and challenges), restricted evidence room (chain-of-custody documents, expert working papers), and counsel-managed claimant intake (privileged/sensitive materials where applicable). Do not guarantee privilege merely by using encryption or labeling a system “legal.”

05 — Procedural fairness makes the research stronger.

For any potentially adverse publication, maintain a clear distinction among official findings, independently modeled estimates, reported allegations and open questions. Offer an evidence correction route and a reasonable right of response. Retain disputed and superseded versions instead of silently editing them. Publish where ownership attribution is uncertain. Identify legitimate regulatory permits and countervailing explanations.

Do not automate filing, public accusations, claimant targeting, or settlement demands from numerical scores. No AI-generated allegation should enter a complaint without competent human legal and scientific review. No leaderboard score is legal evidence by itself.

06 — A scalable delivery sequence.

  1. Source register: ingest time-stamped, versioned official emissions, permit and enforcement data.
  2. Entity resolver: connect company → subsidiary → facility with ownership changes and provenance.
  3. Investigation tickets: public citation issues, independent validation, reproducible source-to-receptor notebooks.
  4. Case intelligence: structured legal screening and scientific uncertainty for each specific harm pathway.
  5. Expert review: panel decisions with conflicts of interest and procedural fairness logs.
  6. Counsel integration: restricted workspaces, authorizations, jurisdiction-specific templates and auditable outcomes.

Public participation should make the records better, not substitute for professional investigation. This site and method are research prototypes and do not provide legal representation or advice.
