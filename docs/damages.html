How the inflation comparison works

For dollar amounts: nominal amount × U.S. CPI-U annual average in 2025 ÷ CPI-U annual average in the disposition year. For Brazilian reais: nominal 2021 amount multiplied by each calendar-year IPCA factor for 2022–2025. These are purchasing-power comparators, not new legal liabilities or a present-value calculation. The 2025 CPI-U annual average omits October 2025, when no CPI survey was collected.

Cash paid over multiple years is deliberately not given one synthetic inflation-adjusted value. Local inflation stays in the original currency; FX conversion is not performed.

What can and cannot be inferred

Paid means a source explicitly documents a completed payment. Partially paid means a dated source reports completed disbursements, without establishing the current final balance. Total unverified means a settlement or order is documented but actual paid-to-date totals have not been independently reconciled.

Penalties, cleanup commitments, personal-injury compensation, natural-resource damages and buybacks address different remedies. A settlement may disclaim liability; a guilty plea is different. Overlapping agreements, multiple defendant entities, different years and future installments must not be added together. Missing paid figures are unknown, not zero.

Read documented event pathways, the scientific methodology and the company evidence explorer before comparing entities.
