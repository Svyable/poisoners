How the inflation comparison works

For dollar amounts: nominal amount × U.S. CPI-U annual average in 2025 ÷ CPI-U annual average in the disposition year. For Brazilian reais: nominal BRL is multiplied by the national IBGE IPCA calendar-year December-to-December annual factors after the nominal year through 2025, including historical 2001–2025 factors to reprice completed Petrobras payments from 2000. The Vale 2021 convention remains a calendar-year reference, not contractual indexation. For Canadian dollars and Indian rupees: original-currency nominal amount times the national annual-average CPI ratio between 2025 and the disposition year. These are purchasing-power comparators, not new legal liabilities or a present-value calculation. The 2025 CPI-U annual average omits October 2025, when no CPI survey was collected.

When an agreement is dated after the 2025 reference year, no 2025 purchasing-power comparator is shown without the relevant full-year index. Historical cash reported for selected quarters or across overlapping agreements is identified separately from cumulative cash. Some paid figures are lower-bound net receipts by a named recipient rather than nationwide or gross company totals; any such basis is shown explicitly and never added to the company's settlement fund contribution. Where cash is verified in one known year, the paid-dollar comparator uses that payment year, not the agreement year. Where each installment is independently documented and the installments reconcile to the paid principal, the comparator is the sum of each cash-year-adjusted installment. Otherwise no synthetic multiyear inflation value is given. Local inflation stays in the original currency; FX conversion is not performed.

What can and cannot be inferred

Paid means a source explicitly documents a completed payment, sometimes by a group of companies; it does not verify every individual company's cash share. Partially paid means a dated source reports completed disbursements, without establishing the current final balance. Total unverified means a settlement or order is documented but actual paid-to-date totals have not been independently reconciled.

Penalties, cleanup commitments, personal-injury compensation, natural-resource damages and buybacks address different remedies. A settlement may disclaim liability; a guilty plea is different. Overlapping agreements, multiple defendant entities, different years and future installments must not be added together. Missing paid figures are unknown, not zero.

Read documented event pathways, the scientific methodology and the company evidence explorer before comparing entities.

i

Read the boundary, not just the number. This separate financial ledger distinguishes source-confirmed payments from court orders, future schedules and capped commitments. Monetary outcomes cannot be added into a company harm score or used as a measure of individual injury.
